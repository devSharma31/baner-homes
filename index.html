Questions people ask me

Why should I pay a fee when I can search NoBroker or 99acres myself?

You can, and many people do. My fee pays for filtering out unavailable and misleading listings, negotiating rent or price (usually saving more than the fee), and checking documents so you don't sign something risky.

Is the fee negotiable, or will it change later?

The fee is fixed and written down before the first visit. It won't go up, and there are no extra charges added at the end.

I'm still in another city. Can you help?

Yes. That's what the ₹4,999 remote shortlist is for. You see flats on live video, and I can hold your pick until you travel to Pune. The ₹4,999 is adjusted in your final fee.

Do you show flats outside Baner–Balewadi?

No. I only work here, so the information I give you about societies, traffic and owners is accurate. If you need another area, I'll point you to someone I trust.

Bachelors, pets, non-vegetarian — will owners accept me?

I note each owner's terms on every listing before sending it, so you only see flats where you're welcome.

What if I'm "just exploring" for now?

That's fine. Pick "3+ months / just exploring" in the form. I'll send a short update once a month instead of a shortlist, so nobody wastes time.
